Ingredients and Allergens | Goodgrain fictional restaurant | Version 1 | Effective 2026-01-01

Masala Dosa contains rice, lentils, potato, mustard seed, and curry leaves. It may contain traces of peanuts and tree nuts due to shared preparation surfaces.

Paneer Biryani contains basmati rice, milk-based paneer, spices, and herbs. It contains milk. Shared kitchen cross-contact cannot be ruled out.

Chole Bhature contains chickpeas, wheat flour, yogurt, and spices. It contains wheat and milk. Shared kitchen cross-contact cannot be ruled out.

Please contact the restaurant before ordering if you have an allergy. Ingredient records are informational and the kitchen cannot guarantee allergen safety or absence of cross-contact.
