Delivery FAQ | Goodgrain fictional restaurant | Version 1 | Effective 2026-01-01

Delivery is available within the demo service area. The application simulates order placement and does not connect to a real courier or payment processor.

Estimated preparation time varies by dish and current kitchen volume. This demo does not provide live courier tracking. Check your order status in the application for kitchen progress.

For help with an order, sign in and use the order history or ask the restaurant assistant. Only the account that placed an order can view or cancel it.
