Restaurant Policy | Goodgrain fictional restaurant | Version 1 | Effective 2026-01-01

Orders may be cancelled while the order is confirmed and before preparation begins. Once the kitchen changes the order to PREPARING, cancellation is no longer available through the application.

Menu availability reflects the number of portions currently recorded by the demo kitchen. Availability can change before an order is submitted, and the application rechecks stock at checkout.

All transactions are simulated for this learning project. No payment is collected and no delivery provider is contacted.
